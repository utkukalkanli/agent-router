The signal

Structure in the traces

Claude rows expose bash, file_read, file_edit; GPT rows expose shell_command, apply_patch. Across 1,000 rows the two sets never overlap, so a cross-family swap is an action the log never took. We route only within a family, on call-zero features: trigger, intent, prompt size, image, toolset.

Honesty

What the log can — and cannot — identify

  • Method — Family-restricted propensity screen + outcome regression on downgrade-from-default; 2,000 trajectory bootstraps. Policy locked on train, reported on 313 untouched trajectories.
  • Cache trap — Cached share is inferred from item-level prefix overlap; a model switch resets it and the next call repays the full prefix. Our certified route switches zero times mid-trajectory, so it never pays that penalty.
  • Support — Routed ESS 155.4, minimum target propensity 0.255. Luna has 12 training rows against a floor of 20, so the router never selects it.
  • Known weakness — Quality is an automatic proxy (unresolved tool error, repeated action), not a measured label — and a cheaper early model could have produced a different later context, which our estimate cannot observe.
